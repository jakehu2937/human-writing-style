# Genre profiles

Use the matching note for the requested format. Shared voice and editing rules live in SKILL.md.

## Email or letter

Give the purpose, essential context, and requested action. Use a greeting and closing suited to the relationship.

## Application or cover letter

Connect one or two actual experiences to the role. Let the examples show the user's contribution and interest.

## Report or memo

Start with the finding or decision, then give the evidence needed to assess it.

## Message or chat

Use conversational phrasing. A short acknowledgment can be the whole message.

## Social post or script

Build around a concrete observation or story. Follow the user's speaking rhythm.

## Explanation or study answer

Give the answer and the reasoning needed to follow it. Use a concrete example when it makes the idea easier to understand.

## Academic or technical prose

Keep precise terms, citations, and meaningful qualifications. Explain the idea in plain sentences that the user could also use in conversation.
